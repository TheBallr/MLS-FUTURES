# Model and page review, 2026-10-21 (Zac: "find inefficiencies and other things that may be holding the model back, or UI changes")

Read-only review of the engine (`sim/`), the ratings build, the morning pipeline and the published page (run 20261021T124324Z,
page rendered headless at 1440 and 390 px, every tab, no console errors). Nothing in the model or the page was changed.
Proposals are numbered for Zac's "do N"; the numbers in CLAUDE.md section 19 match these.

## A. What is holding the model back (ranked)

1. **The Edges tab has no book prices.** `ODDS_API_KEY` is not set in the routine's container, so `fetch_odds.py` skips
   (`weekly_steps.json`: "skipped (ODDS_API_KEY not set)"), `data/market/futures_odds.csv` is a header only, `edges.json`
   `n_prices` 0, and the tab is a third copy of our Cup price with dashes. The whole edge workflow (section 16) is idle until
   the key is in. Guard to add when it is: `fetch_odds.py:26-32` maps every unnamed outright to `cup`; map by title and reject
   unknown markets (a Shield or "make playoffs" outright would otherwise land in the Cup field and `edges.py:67` would take the
   longest price as the Cup quote, a spurious flag).
2. **The confidence range the edge rule uses is not an uncertainty range.** `risk.confidence` (`risk.py:279-321`) takes the
   min/max over four recipe alternatives (no recent lines, xG 0%, xG 30%, spread x1.06) at 20k seasons. It leaves out the
   filter's posterior on the rating (sd 0.12-0.13 per club, the 95% band on the Ratings tab), the hand adjustments, and the
   20k-season noise (about 0.3 points at 20%). Today Nashville's Cup range is 22.6-23.6 (1 point) while its +0.06 hand alone is
   worth 1.7 points (14k: 22.9 v 21.2) and a one-sd rating move about 3. The cautious EV on the Edges tab (`edges.py:82`, pooled
   at the low end of this range) will therefore flag "edges" that sit inside rating noise. Proposal: build the range from the
   posterior: in the Risk sim draw the ratings from the filter's posterior (sd per club from `kalman_sd`, hand included) and
   read the 20th and 80th percentile of each price; keep the recipe alternatives as a second line. The Bayesian notes
   (section 18) ask for the same thing.
3. **Knockout draws after 90 minutes are a coin flip in every round.** `sim/playoffs.knockout` gives a drawn match to a coin.
   The Wild Card and Round One go straight to penalties (right). The conference semis, conference finals and MLS Cup play 30
   minutes of extra time first, where the stronger side keeps its edge. Size (engine at T 3.3, dc -0.05): a favourite at
   +1.04 goals (a +0.60 club at home) wins 71.6% of the match under the coin, 74.1% with a 30-minute extra period at a third of
   the goal means then a coin (+2.5 points); at +0.44 it is +1.2, at +0.74 +1.8. Three such matches stand between a top seed
   and the Cup, so the favourites' Cup and Reach MLS Cup prices run 1-2 points low, the underdogs' high. Proposal: extra time
   as a 30-minute mini-match (means x 1/3, same shared component scaled, no home edge change) before the coin, in the semis,
   finals and the Cup only; test update in `tests/run_tests.py`; report the paired 200k effect.
4. **Ratings too flat this season.** Risk tile Spread 1.10 (close on our week-out ratings, last 148 games; 15-season normal
   1.02; bias +0.04 on the home side), warn for days. 14j found the same thing another way (30% carry-over of the last-4
   miss). Favourites' futures run low when the slope is above 1. Nothing is proposed blind: test a season-level spread
   multiplier (x1.05, x1.10 on the blend) on the rest-of-season backtest and on 2026 close-tracking, and separately whether
   the +0.04 home bias is the frozen 0.44 against 2026 closes that imply 0.45-0.47 (`he_table`: 0.468 Jul, 0.451 Aug, 0.447 Sep,
   0.439 Oct). Adopt only on the usual rule (wins most seasons, Zac's word).
5. **Measurement sds not set on evidence.** `kalman_s_posted` 0.22 treats a posted line (days before kickoff) as more precise
   than a close (`kalman_s` 0.25); the config note cites no test. Every close enters at 0.25 whatever its source: the 34
   market-average closes and the 10-minute captures too. Proposal: grid s_posted (0.18-0.35) on the lag-0 close-tracking
   yardstick, and give `avg_close` rows a larger sd (0.30-0.35) on the lag-7 yardstick.
6. **Forecast check and CLV log are in sample** (section 4 item 4, still open). `run_sim.py:172-176` scores `g_model`, built
   from ratings that already carry this round's posted lines, against those lines: today rmse 0.061 v the honest 0.24.
   `clv_log.py:19-21` logs disagree/attack from the same number. `layers_log.csv` is appended on `--quick`/`--no-write` runs
   (`run_sim.py:178-183`), and `update_clv` on `--quick`. Fix: score the no-lines ratings (`site_data.py:63-67` builds them)
   and guard both logs. Nothing on the page reads these, but the run line and `run_summary.json` mislead.
7. **`what_if.py xg_w` drops the hand** (section 4 item 2, open): `what_if.py:51-55` rebuilds the ratings from the sources and
   never adds `hand_vec`; `risk.py:287` has the right recipe. Any xg_w what-if is priced without MIA/NSH/SEA/LAFC's hands.
8. **Four recipes for "ratings without this round's lines" and two "Pinnacle-implied rating" definitions.** No-lines:
   `site_data.py:63-67`, `site_detail.py:204-218`, `risk.py:188-195`, `risk.py:291-294` (equivalent today, by luck: the
   expansion prior is inert and `he_at(asof)` 0.439 v the knob 0.44). Implied: `site_data._implied` (:107-110, raw close, no
   factors) feeds the Ratings grid and the Morning "least confident" card; `site_detail.py:169-174` (net of factors) feeds
   History. For an eliminated or depleted club the raw window sits about 0.19 low (Morning patches only the eliminated case).
   One function for each, net of factors, used everywhere.
9. **The close-tracking and backtest "live" baseline is the retired least-squares recipe** (section 4 item 3, open):
   `close_track.py:34`, `backtest_season.py:52` (hl 28, ridge 0.5, xG 25%), the default `--baseline`. Any new "v live"
   comparison is against a dead model; the Kalman recipes there also run without the club home edges. Set the default recipe
   to the live full-health Kalman build and record dc_rho and the dock in the sim-stage rows.
10. **Data checks that cannot fire.** (a) `fetch_psr.py:221` re-stamps `updated` = today on every row the old report still
    lists (100 of 148 rows carry 2026-10-21 from a report published 10-16), so the Risk "injury rows not refreshed in 3 weeks"
    check (`risk.py:420-425`) never fires for psr rows. (b) `absence_values.py:118` never marks a row stale once it has an
    expected return date (Transfermarkt fills blanks), so a player who has played keeps his cost; 0 rows today, but it is the
    one path by which a stale absence reaches a price. (c) `risk.py:43` `PINNACLE_BOOKS` omits `pinnacle_lastposted`, so the
    first missed capture is dropped from the tracking tile and counted as "no close". (d) `weekly.py:47` keys both
    `fetch_asa_players` calls on one name: a failed 2025 fetch (the 2026-10-19 bug) is invisible. (e) `freshness.py` runs
    before morning/build_site/check_site, so "all steps ran" never sees those three fail.
11. **risk.py takes 173 s because the dock pre-pass runs ~30 times** (`confidence()`: base, four alternatives, one +0.10 bump
    per club with a live price; each `Sim.run` repeats `dock_exempt_from_prepass`, 20k seasons). Compute the exemption once
    for the base ratings and reuse it; bump only the clubs on the watchlist or Edges list. Halves the step or better.
12. **Dead knobs and dead switches.** `half_life_days` (only the off layers read it), `injury_tiers`/`injury_layer_enabled`,
    `callup_layer_enabled`, `kalman_openers`/`kalman_s_open`/`kalman_xg_sd`, `ridge_fade_mw`, `shade`, `clv_attack_threshold`,
    `xg_goal_mix`: all off or inert. They stay in `config.json` and two of them drive page features (Injuries "Tier raw",
    Settings sliders). Mark them "retired" in config and drop them from the page; `refresh_ratings.py` docstring still says WLS.
13. **Smaller engine items, recorded, not proposed:** playoff knockouts use the league home edge (0.44) rather than the host's
    club delta (RSL +0.05, LAFC +0.05: 0.3-0.5 points on a home semi); the season draw is independent N(0, 0.10) per club
    while the filter's posterior is correlated and 0.13 (tails 22% in the 20% bands, `kf_final_sim`); the World Cup pause is
    treated as an off-season (25% reversion on Jul 16, measured better, section 13); in-sim eliminations are docked 0.5 while
    clubs already out get only the factor -0.19 (consistent with 14e); `latest_posted` has no upper bound on `fetched_at`, so a
    past `--asof` sees lines fetched later (live run unaffected); the fixture builder is duplicated in `run_sim.py:128-154`
    and `what_if.py:85-102` (identical output today). The 16 MB `h2h` arrays (two (N,30,30) int8) are fine at 200k.

## B. The page (ranked by what helps a bet decision)

14. **The tab dots are always on (bug).** `.tabdot{display:inline-block}` (`site/index.html:175`) beats the `hidden`
    attribute, so the Morning, Edges and Risk dots show whatever the flags say: Edges shows a green dot with 0 flags today.
    Fix: `.tabdot[hidden]{display:none}` and drop the hard-coded `s-ok` on `#edDot` (:235).
15. **No "today" screen.** The page opens on Ratings (or the last tab); the morning decision (any edge, is the data current,
    what moved a point, which hands are in force, which prices are wide) is spread over Morning (six cards, 3,262 px), Edges,
    Risk (five cards) and Ratings. Proposal: Morning as the default tab with one short "Today" card on top: edge flags (count
    and the best), the data verdict in one line, hands in force with the keep/trim verdict, futures moves of a point or more,
    the widest ranges; Data checks collapsed to one line when all ok; the by-month track record behind a hover.
16. **Edges tab until the books land:** hide the empty Market/Best/Edge/EV/Cautious columns and the flag-rule essay; once
    prices arrive put best price v cautious fair first on the first screen. Show the range beside every live price on the
    Ratings tab (as the Edges tab does); make the Risk dot amber unless a health tile is red (15 flags today, so a red dot
    every day says nothing).
17. **One name per pool, one unit.** `cup_finalist` is "Reach MLS Cup" on Ratings/Conferences/Risk/History and "East/West
    champion" on Edges; regular-season first is "Conf 1st" on three tabs and "Seed 1" on Conferences. Ratings and Risk show
    percentages, Conferences American odds, Edges both. Proposal: one label per pool everywhere and one global %/American
    toggle (remembered), the other unit on hover.
18. **Hands shown in four places with conflicting cues.** Ratings Hand boxes, Data checks, Risk blind spots, Risk watchlist;
    the week selector reads "Week of Oct 20 - Oct 26 · current · not saved yet", which reads as "not in the run" when the
    hands are in it (carried from the week of Oct 13). Label it "carried from week of Oct 13"; keep one hand summary (the
    Data-checks row with the verdict).
19. **Stale text.** Risk blind spot "Injury layer off: absences reach the ratings only through Pinnacle's closes, so a fresh
    injury shows up one game late" (`risk.py:475`) is wrong since full-health ratings went on. Injuries "Tier raw" column is
    the dead tier layer. Settings: Drift and Randomness sliders are frozen knobs the page reports and never applies (drop the
    tab, move the xG-share slider to Ratings). Risk "Rating changes" subtitle is in UTC, every other stamp ET.
20. **Phone (390 px) is not usable for the decision.** Tab bar scrolls with History, Risk, Injuries and Settings off-screen
    and no indicator; the Refreshed/Last close stamp is hidden; the Ratings table is 1,072 px wide in a 356 px card with no
    sticky team column; the hand arrows are 18 x 12 px. Proposal: sticky first column, default phone columns Rating + Reach
    MLS Cup + Win MLS Cup (the Columns menu exists), a wrapped tab bar. Desktop at 1440: the Conferences cards clip the Bottom
    column and the Games detail row overflows 64 px (fine at 1280 and 1680; raise the stacking breakpoint to ~1500).
21. **Settled columns and long lists.** Conf 1st and Playoffs are 100/0 for all but about eight clubs: grey or hide settled
    columns by default. History "What changed it" lists every run including zero moves (page 4,914 px): filter to moves of
    0.01 or a logged change. Morning "Least confident" uses bare codes while every other table has crest + name; Games "Gap"
    is in points and Morning "Gap" in goals under the same word.
